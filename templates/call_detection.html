How do we detect scam calls?

Our system uses a three-layer detection mechanism:

  • 1. Blacklist Check: We cross-reference the number against a database of confirmed reports.
  • 2. Prefix Analysis: We identify calls from high-risk international regions known for fraud (e.g., +234, +92).
  • 3. Pattern Recognition: We look for suspicious structural patterns, such as unnatural repetition of digits (e.g. 555555), which often indicate spoofed IDs.
